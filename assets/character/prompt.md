# Иллюстрация преподавателя

Создано встроенным инструментом Imagegen, с `transparent_background: true`. Это вымышленный персонаж, не фотография владельца сайта.

## Финальный промпт

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: standalone transparent character illustration for the hero of a personal mathematics tutor website.
Create an original premium cartoon illustration. A fictional adult male educator, around his thirties, friendly confident expression, natural facial proportions with slightly larger expressive head, warm light complexion, dark short slightly tousled hair, round subtle dark glasses. Sculpted refined 3D cartoon aesthetic with soft clay-like materials and subtle studio lighting, crisp clean contours, editorial character design appealing to older students and parents.
Contemporary understated charcoal loose knit sweater over a restrained off-white shirt, a single very small cobalt blue accent such as a pen. Waist-up to mid-thigh framing, entire head and both hands completely visible, one hand holding a small off-white notebook against the body, the other relaxed holding a marker. Carefully formed natural hands. Face directed toward the viewer, relaxed confident posture and subtle asymmetry, central figure with enough transparent margin around the complete silhouette, legible at large desktop scale and mobile.
Native genuinely transparent background with a real alpha channel. No text, no letters, no formulas, no logos, no watermark, no surrounding scene, no ground plane, no glow. Do not imitate any real person or existing branded character. Portrait image, high resolution approximately 1600–2400px tall.
```

## Файлы

- `tutor.png`: исходное изображение RGBA, 1024 × 1536 px, настоящий альфа-канал.
- `tutor-640.webp`: оптимизированная версия 640 × 960 px, с альфа-каналом.
- `tutor-1200.webp`: версия 1200 × 1800 px, с альфа-каналом. Масштабирована из оригинала; новых деталей не добавляет.
- `avatar.webp`: кроп той же иллюстрации, 360 × 400 px.

WebP и аватар подготовлены Pillow: изменение размера и кадрирование. Содержание оригинальной иллюстрации не изменялось. Края проверены на светлом и тёмном фоне.
